Site prep · Mega-Q system · Nov 19 2026

Locked system: 1 Daikin Mega-Q RXHWQ120MQTJA + BWLP120TJU (bought from Daikin), 2 TEHQ 700 gal thermal storage tanks (2nd tank quote pending), 10 kW immersion backup, dual-coil CSST HX, Solera pump group PPPL0722.01, temp/level sensor PPTS0223.02, steel rack. Daily max 3,330 GPD, one Mega-Q covers it (Garth, Nov 19). Every value is marked SPEC (from a spec sheet, cited) or ASSUMED · verify (verify with the engineer, Garth or Daikin). Not for construction.

C. To confirm before drawings

  1. Garth / TEHQ: 2nd 700 gal tank, plus its BOS/temp-level sensor kit and 2nd dual-coil HX: EST69 shows only 1 of each (the Nov 4 BOM had 2). Updated quote pending.
  2. Garth / TEHQ: Immersion elements: EST69 has 2 x 10 kW. The model puts 1 in each tank. Need voltage/phase, controls and high-limit.
  3. Garth / TEHQ + Daikin: TEHQ tank is atmospheric, but Daikin calls for a closed-type tank system. The model has the Mega-Q heating the tanks through the CSST coil in a closed, pressurized loop with an expansion tank. Confirm.
  4. Daikin: Daikin needs 1 tank controller kit (BRP26B2VJU) per tank, so 2 kits. They are not on EST69.
  5. Daikin: The Mega-Q is an R410A / R134a cascade, not CO2 (R744), per the submittal.
  6. Daikin: HSU gas line is 1-1/8 in on the submittal vs 7/8 in in the design manual. Need the max line length HSU to cascade.
  7. Daikin: HSU front air discharge faces a 3 ft-0 in open aisle with the cascade unit (5 ft) and pump group (6 ft assumed) beyond it. Confirm the clearance pattern.
  8. Garth / TEHQ: Solera pump group size, weight, electrical data and connection sizes (model uses 4 x 2 x 6 ft, 550 lb, all assumed).
  9. Garth / TEHQ: TEHQ "steel rack dunnage" ($12,000) vs the existing Option C L-platform used in the model.
  10. Engineer (EOR): Weights: total operating 17,716 lb; I-beam 1 8,184 lb, I-beam 2 6,660 lb, posts 2,872 lb, max beam utilization 0.65 (simplified split). Coil, element, pump group and piping weights are assumed.
  11. Engineer (MEP): Electrical service capacity, feeder size and route, roof panel location; DHW/CW/recirc pipe sizes from the Precision plumbing plans; glycol (none assumed); insulation.
